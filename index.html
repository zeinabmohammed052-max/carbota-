⚙️ Pneumatic & Bio-Spraying Mechanism

01

Gas Expansion & Regulation (2 Bars)

Liquefied CO2 (~55 bars) expands into gas through a mini-regulator set at 2 bars followed by a check valve.

02

Gas Displacement (5.56g CO2 / 1L Liquid)

By Boyle's Law (P1V1=P2V2), 1L liquid at 2 bars uses 5.56g CO2. A 16g capsule pushes 2.88L (~60 m²); a 2L cylinder pushes 242L (1 Feddan).

03

Solenoid Valve & Servo Boom

A 3W valve (Mini) or 10W Brass Valve (Large) opens for 2s bursts while an MG996R servo sweeps up to 50cm.

04

Micron Atomization & CO2 Boost

0.3mm brass nozzles atomize Neem Oil & K-Soap while released CO2 boosts leaf photosynthesis.
